Privacy Policy — Conversation Bridge

Last updated: September 2026

Conversation Bridge is a browser extension that saves, organises and exports AI chat conversations. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

What the extension accesses

  • Chat pages you open on chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com and perplexity.ai. The extension reads the conversation text shown on the page only when you use one of its features (capture, copy, save, transfer, live sync).
  • Your clipboard, only to copy text you asked to copy.
  • Your Downloads folder, only to save files you asked to export.

Where your data is stored

Saved conversations, notes, tags, workflows, prompts and settings are stored locally in your browser profile using chrome.storage.local. They are never uploaded to any server operated by us. You can export a full backup or delete everything at any time from the Settings tab.

Network requests

The extension makes no network requests of its own. There is no licensing, update check, or any other call to a server operated by us.

The “Transfer” feature opens a new tab on the assistant you choose and pastes text into its composer. From that point on, the assistant’s own privacy policy applies to what you send.

Analytics and tracking

None. The extension contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising or fingerprinting code.

Permissions explained

  • storage, unlimitedStorage — keep your library on your device.
  • scripting and host permissions for the chat sites — read the conversation on the page and paste into the composer.
  • sidePanel — show the panel.
  • clipboardWrite — copy exports.
  • downloads — save exports to your Downloads folder.
  • contextMenus — right-click “Save” / “Copy” actions.
