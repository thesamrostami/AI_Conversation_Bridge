Conversation Bridge is ready

Save, search, export and hand off your AI chats — entirely on your machine.

1

Open a chat

Go to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Perplexity and open any conversation.

2

Click the icon

Pin Conversation Bridge to the toolbar and click it. The side panel reads the chat instantly.

3

Copy, save or transfer

Export clean Markdown, keep a searchable library, or send the context to another assistant.

Open ChatGPT Open Claude Open Gemini Open Perplexity

What you get

  • Clean Markdown with code fences, tables, lists, links and math preserved
  • Markdown, plain text, JSON, HTML and PDF exports
  • A local library with full-text search, tags, notes and favorites
  • One-click hand-off of a chat to another assistant
  • Workflows that bundle related chats into a shared context packet
  • Prompt templates you can append to any export or transfer
  • Backups and imports — including official ChatGPT and Claude exports
  • Everything stays in your browser. No account, no server, no tracking.

Keyboard shortcuts

Alt/⌥ + Shift + BOpen the side panel
Alt/⌥ + Shift + CCopy the current chat as Markdown
Alt/⌥ + Shift + SSave the current chat to the library

Shortcuts can be changed at chrome://extensions/shortcuts. Tip: right-click any chat page for quick “Save” and “Copy” actions.

Upgrading from an earlier version? Your saved conversations, prompts and workflows were migrated automatically.
